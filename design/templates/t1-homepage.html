Primary landing page that routes visitors to key content areas. Displays dynamic updates, news cards, and social media integration.

Read me first

The purpose of this template is to demonstrate the design system of the new website, and also consolidate our various use cases across both languages. You will find T1 to T9 templates across the site.

During review, do not drill into content, function, authentication or presentation structure.
However, the font and icon set are not final, and you are welcome to provide feedback.

In providing feedback or questions, please specify the template number, e.g. T1-Homepage. You may contact Deacon Michael Chau with your feedback.

✅Review these:

  • Is the color suitable?
  • Is the text readable?
  • Is it easy to navigate on both desktop and mobile?
  • Is there some content you want to present but not able to fit into any of the T1–T9 templates?
  • Is there any layout/design/UI component that you would like to include or replace?

❌ Not these

  • Why is this sermon outdated?
  • Why can't I switch between languages?
  • Can we not use this subtitle?
  • Why is this link broken?
  • Why is this event form not working?
  • Why don't I need to log in to the member area?
  • Can we not use this photo as the head banner?

請先看這裡

此模板的目的是展示新網站的設計系統(Design System)，同時整合我們在中英文兩種語言中的各種使用情境。您可以在整個網站中找到 T1 至 T9 的模板。

審閱時，請不要深究內容、功能、身份驗證或呈現結構。
然而, 字型及Icon set 也不是最終定案, 非常歡迎提供意見。

在提供意見或疑問時, 請註明模板編號, 例如 T1-Homepage. 可聯絡執事 Michael Chau 提供意見。

✅ Review these:

  • 顏色合適嗎？
  • 文字容易閱讀嗎？
  • 在桌面版和手機版上都容易導航嗎？
  • 我想呈現某些內容或事工，但T1-T9 任何模板都不適合？
  • 有沒有您想加入或替換的版面/設計/UI 元件？
  • 在設計中加入其他元素? 例如節期、背誦經文

Design System Palette
顏色系統

Designer of this template is someone with deuteranomaly. We welcome your feedback and contribution.
This template inherits the main color scheme of the existing website and adjusts it to be less saturated, also adopting a simple design style.

設計這份模板的人有紅綠色盲。歡迎提供意見。
此模板承傳現有網站的主色調再加以調整, 令飽和度更低, 也採取簡約的設計風格。

Blue #2E5A85 Primary brand colour — header, headings, buttons, links
Cream #FFF6B3 Secondary accent — highlights, badges, soft backgrounds
Coral #F07A60 CTA / action — accent buttons, progress bars, alerts
White #FFFFFF Surfaces — cards, modals, text on dark backgrounds
Off-white #F7F8FA Page background — the base canvas behind all content

Templates 模板

T1

Homepage / Aggregator

Primary landing page that routes visitors to key content areas. Displays dynamic updates, news cards, and social media integration.
T2

Simple Content Layout

General-purpose informational page for narrative content, program descriptions, event details, and announcements.
T2-ZH

簡約內容版面（繁體中文）

通用資訊頁面，適用於敘述性內容、事工介紹、活動詳情及公告。
T2-Dark

Simple Content Layout (Dark Theme)

Dark theme variant of T2. Same structure — text blocks, split content, lists — rendered on a dark surface for evening reading or dramatic visual tone.
T3

Grid / Collection Layout

Displays categorized listings, directories, and archives as a responsive card grid. Ideal for ministries, news, and blog indexes.
T4

Media (Audio) Archive

Specialized listing and playback for audio and video content. Each card includes an embedded player with notes and download actions.
T5

Team Profiles

Showcases people with circular headshots, names, roles, and bios. Used for pastoral staff, ministry teams, and leadership directories.
T6

Form / Transaction

Conversion-focused page for data capture, registration, authentication, and financial transactions. No hero banner — intentionally minimal.
T7

Member Area

Authenticated member dashboard with governance info, documents, and quick links. Requires login to access.
T8

Event List

Instagram-style event card grid. Shows title, date range, time, location, and fee at a glance. Links to event detail pages.
T9

News List

Weekly worship bulletins and church announcements. Stay informed about what's happening at Westside Baptist Church.
