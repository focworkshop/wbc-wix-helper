Greetings in the Name of Our Lord Jesus Christ

On behalf of Westside Baptist Church, I would like to welcome you personally to our church's web page and am thrilled to have you visit with us through this media. We pray that you will get to know us more through its content.

Westside Baptist is not just a church. We're a Christian community, a Christian family. Westside is a community of committed, contagious and contemporary young Christians. The purpose of our existence is to win people to Christ, to help them grow in maturity and to equip them to serve Him as a community.

We invite those who are interested in finding meaning about life, maturity in Christ, and those looking for a church home in Vancouver to come visit us. You'll find a warm, welcoming community that loves God and loves people.

Our Sunday Services

Every Sunday we gather together for worship, learning, and fellowship. We offer services in both English and Cantonese to serve our diverse community.

  • English Service: 9:00 AM
  • Cantonese Service: 11:00 AM
  • Sunday School: 10:15 AM
  • Children's Program: During all services

What to Expect

Our worship style is contemporary, with a band and praise team leading a wide selection of songs ranging from modern to traditional. We use multimedia equipment such as visual aids, video, and digital pictures in worship, but we recognize that the key to worship is in spirit and truth.

Street parking is available around the church. We've prepared a parking map with helpful information for first-time visitors.

Our Mission: W.E.S.T. and S.I.D.E.

Worshipping God in a culturally relevant context. Evangelizing through one's lifestyle and the whole Christian community. Sharing our love through small groups. Teaching the Word of God as the authority of life.

Sending people into the world to fulfill the greatest commission. Integrating faith into everyday life and being His witnesses. Developing new models of ministries strategically for the furtherance of His kingdom. Equipping the laity to do the work of the ministries for the functioning of the whole body.
