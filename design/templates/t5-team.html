Rev. Dr. Gladys Lee Tsang

Senior Pastor · Founding Pastor

Rev. Dr. Gladys Lee Tsang was called by the Canadian Baptist denomination to lead the church planting effort that would become Westside Baptist Church. In September 1990, the church was officially planted among the Chinese community on the west side of Vancouver.

On the same day that Westside was officially chartered as a congregation in 1992, Gladys was ordained as pastor. She has led the church through remarkable growth and transformation over three decades, including the purchase of the church building at 8506 Ash Street in 2004, the launch of the English congregation in 2012, and the development of ministries to serve Hong Kong immigrants beginning in 2021.

Under her leadership, Westside Baptist has grown from a small church plant into a vibrant, multicultural community that serves the Marpole neighbourhood and beyond.

Rev. Anders Tsang

Associate Pastor (Retired)

Rev. Anders Tsang finished his studies at Regent College and felt the call to pastoral ministry. He was called as Associate Pastor at Westside Baptist in 2001 and was ordained in 2004.

Throughout his tenure, Anders served faithfully alongside the pastoral team, contributing to preaching, pastoral care, and the administration of church life. He officially retired in 2019 and has since served as interim pastor when needed, demonstrating his ongoing commitment to the Westside family.

Rev. Dr. Alan C. Yu

Pastor

Rev. Dr. Alan C. Yu has been a long-time member of Westside Baptist Church. In 2009, he began serving as a part-time pastoral intern while continuing his theological education.

Recognizing his gifts and calling, the church called Alan as the third full-time pastor in 2011. He was ordained in 2014. Alan has been instrumental in preaching, teaching, and leading various ministries including the sermon series on kingdom principles that has been a cornerstone of the church's teaching ministry.

Pastor Mark Wong

English & Youth Ministry Pastor

Pastor Mark Wong first joined Westside Baptist as a pastoral intern. After gaining experience and completing his pastoral preparation, he returned to Westside and was called as the fourth full-time pastor in 2022.

Mark serves primarily in the English congregation and youth ministry. His energy, relatability, and passion for the next generation have been vital to the growth of the English-language ministry and youth programs at Westside Baptist.
