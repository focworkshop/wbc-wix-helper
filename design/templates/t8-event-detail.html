Description

Join us for an unforgettable five-day summer camp experience at Camp Squeah in the beautiful Fraser Valley! This camp is designed for youth in grades 7–12 and is packed with outdoor adventures, worship sessions, small group discussions, and tons of fun.

Activities include hiking, canoeing, rock climbing, campfire worship, drama performances, and team challenges. Our theme this year is "Rooted" — exploring what it means to build a life grounded in faith.

All meals and accommodations are included. Transportation by chartered bus from the church is provided. Campers should bring sleeping bags, personal toiletries, and weather-appropriate clothing.
